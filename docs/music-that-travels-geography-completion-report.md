## New evidence precision and types

Of 87 newly accepted queue records, first overview anchors have **66 city, 5 region, 16 country** precision. Counted individual source claims across those records: **83 artist, 11 recording, 2 repertoire/cultural**. A claim count is not an extra located record; multiple claims can apply to one release.

Source URLs and scope caveats are stored per case in `source/geography-completion-review-register.json`; authoritative runtime inputs are in `source/cultural-threads.json`. One Discogs artist ID can benefit multiple exact matching editions. In collaborations, the claim applies only to the identified contributor. Compilation concepts are not used to manufacture geographic origins for their constituent tracks.

### Particularly useful research discoveries

- Codek's *Closer / Tam Tam*: exact original Studio d'Auteuil and Centre Georges Pompidou sessions in Paris, based on label liner notes.
- *What Makes You Go For It*: the instrumental formed in Cologne and Prosumer's title-track vocals were added later in Berlin, according to Ostgut Ton. Different production stages are shown separately.
- Stephen Encinas's *Disco Illusion*: original local-band recording in Trinidad and Tobago, not the later reissue country.
- Crystal Waters's *Gypsy Woman*: underlying original recording and mix at the Basement Boys studio, Baltimore; **partial** claim rather than blanket remix coverage.
- DJ Slip's *No Satellites Please*: original pressing/label explicitly credits production in Minneapolis.
- Will DiMaggio's *At Ease*: exact album recorded in Ridgewood, Queens.
- DJ Marcelle's *For*: the original release's distributor explicitly states Amsterdam recording.
- Helena Hauff's *Have You Been There, Have You Seen It*: recorded in her Hamburg studio.
- Roberto Musci & Giovanni Venosta's *Urban and Tribal Portraits*: archival release credits localise the original winter 1987–88 sessions to Milan.
- *Totally Wired*: original compilation curated for the London club scene, without falsely locating all sampled/compiled tracks there.
- Cheb Runner's *Tagnawit*: Brussels electronic artist base and Moroccan Gnawa tradition explicitly distinguished.
