## What changed in the method

- Album, mixed LP, unofficial pressing, EP, single, remix and 12-inch remain distinct. A 12-inch is never automatically an EP. The generic planner emits format/provenance-specific research questions without guessing fame.
- Non-geographic findings are retained separately from map pins, so production techniques, repertoire and contributor roles need not be forced into a city. Version checks have a separate value and cannot inflate meaningful-context totals.
- Accepted collection connections require both owned edition IDs, exact identities, role evidence for both endpoints, a relationship type and dated read sources. A different collection omits links with absent endpoints.
- The Mancini & The Creepers release is quarantined from the wrongly linked Paris producer. Its Bristol/London context is release-scoped until the vocalist identity is corrected.
- Shared evidence is sent once and hydrated on load. The map retains sources, notes, supporting claims and track scope; the API is 682,284 bytes versus the earlier 694,399 bytes, despite the extra research. The full ledger never ships to the browser.

## Useful examples

- **Nuova Napoli:** Naples is a musical reference and home scene; NG Tower Studios in Berlin is explicitly credited for the recording.
- **Directstep:** original 1978 Tokyo direct-to-disc recording; 2019 replica pressing credits are not treated as manufacturing evidence.
- **Syn:** Vienna main sessions, with Chicago limited to additional drums on Odessa and Moon (A1/A4).
- **Ahyewa Special:** Ghana Films Studio, country-level evidence. Kumasi belongs to the original label history, not a guessed studio city.
- **Midnight is Comin’:** Yukimatsu is curator/sequencer of twelve named producer contributions, rather than sole composer of every track.
- **My Girlfriend:** detailed São Paulo live/party history, despite an initial no-master/ambiguous selection proxy.
- **Music People:** the Mood Hut collaboration is not automatically connected to Moodymann’s track with that title.

## Documented collection connections

- George Clinton appears on Kendrick’s Wesley’s Theory and leads the owned Do Fries Go With That Shake release. The latter recording is not claimed as the sampled source.
- Ron Trent writes/produces Humans Drums & Machines and is executive producer on ABCF; Jared Hines remains ABCF’s credited producer.
- Giusto Pio’s arrangements connect L’Arca Di Noè and La Voce del Padrone; the albums remain distinct recording sessions.
- Nuova Napoli and Bar Mediterraneo credit Andrea De Fazio and Fabiana Martone. The shared musicians form a specific bridge across the Nu Guinea/Nu Genea name change.

These are contributor bridges, not automatic samples, identical recordings or shared sessions. The Headhunters-related single has no sufficient player credits in the reviewed edition; no edge is manufactured.

## Remaining limits

Only **3/50** have all three source routes closed. All fifty have an explicit route outcome, but many archive/official routes remain unavailable or undiscovered. A supported narrow finding can stop a bounded review while those gaps stay open. No archive was declared exhaustively searched. The third batch is not 50 fully researched musical histories.

Seven editions remain below the stronger context threshold:
- **Atom Heart Mother** (8872804): Version and identity checks completed, but this pass has not established a new meaningful musical context beyond the existing map or credited roles. Official shop redirects to a password page; existing Abbey Road evidence is preserved but is not counted as a newly verified studio claim.
- **Bad Girl (BT Remixes)** (205746): Version and identity checks completed, but this pass has not established a new meaningful musical context beyond the existing map or credited roles. Official biography unavailable and no remix-session location verified; Singapore birthplace is not a musical-origin shortcut.
- **Infiltrate / Work It (Remixes)** (168868): The drum-and-bass remixer remains unidentified. Neither Kingston nor Portsmouth Virginia can stand in for the bootleg producer’s working location.
- **Smile ** (7972280): Version and identity checks completed, but this pass has not established a new meaningful musical context beyond the existing map or credited roles. Berlin/Chicago artist profile locations do not establish a shared recording session.
- **Elephant** (453412): No directly documented scene/session context found beyond credits. Version names do not establish African origins or a Burundi recording.
- **Free Rythms Dance** (14460113): Need a sleeve or archive link between Annick Nozati/Christiane de Rougemont’s association and this specific instrumental ensemble before placing the record in Paris.
- **This Time EP Sampler** (680278): Version and identity checks completed, but this pass has not established a new meaningful musical context beyond the existing map or credited roles. No recording location verified; producer biographies do not resolve mix-specific credits automatically.

The initial “less documented” and “obscure” hypotheses were qualified after source review without changing cohort membership. Nu Guinea, Innode, My Girlfriend and Gerd illustrate why familiarity, missing masters and evidence quality must remain separate. The remaining established-album pool did not contain unreviewed explicitly Blues-tagged albums; existing blues cases were retained in earlier batches rather than repeated or relabelled.

## Timing and comparison

Discogs collection: 170 unique resources, 3 cache hits, 512.104 seconds including public-API pacing and network time. Four additional owned endpoints were later read for connection review. These are metadata collection measurements, not total research effort.

The timestamped review journal is partial. It includes source/tool latency, one cross-context window also contains implementation, and some follow-up reads fell outside windows. Therefore it cannot support a total review-time figure, minutes per category or comparison with the uninstrumented earlier batch. This is an instrumentation limitation to fix in the next intake, not evidence of faster research.

Both 50-edition cohorts yield 43 meaningful contexts, but their composition and expanded context model differ. Do not read this as a controlled improvement. A wholly independent collection is still needed to test end-to-end reuse and completeness.
