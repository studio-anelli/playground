### What the evidence can and cannot establish

**Identity:** exact release and artist IDs have been reviewed, but identity exceptions and aliases can still need work. **Geography:** recording place, musical scene, edition market and later production stages are separated; a missing recording address does not make artist-scene context invalid. **Context:** meaningful-context flag measures a supported initial finding, not history exhaustiveness. **Connections:** four accepted cross-record contributor bridges are not proof of an exhaustive sample/collaboration search. **Evidence:** unavailable, indexed-only and harvested-only sources are not successful factual confirmation.

Do not mark uninvestigated dimensions as disproven or absent. `not_applicable` source-stage outcomes are not missing work. A source-route being unavailable is not equivalent to failure of a separately supported narrow claim.

## Pass D — priorities and stopping rules

- **P0 factual integrity:** conflicts, wrong artist identities, unverified precise geography, or version/track attribution errors. No correction without exact source and scope.
- **P1 documented missing context:** the 14 below-threshold editions; work first from existing questions and unresolved source routes.
- **P2 promising connections:** high-impact musician/sample/cover/network leads found by cross-collection comparison, with release-specific evidence.
- **P3 depth:** recordings with valid scene context but thin session histories; research only if available evidence may genuinely improve the map or collection story.

A bounded case can stop when a decisive source supports its narrow conclusion, with open routes and limits retained. Do not increase yield by converting label addresses, artist nationality, mastering locations or generic genre tags into musical-origin pins.
