# AUDIT-02 — Independent source challenge of 20 selected editions

Reviewed 2026-11-09. Follows AUDIT-01 sample without changing selections. This is a **targeted web-source check**, not an exhaustive discography audit or audio-fingerprint analysis. Search results that repeat a catalogue are not counted as independent confirmation of sessions. Sources listed below were inspected through public search retrieval; inaccessible or missing source routes remain explicit. New possible relationships are **leads only** and have not been written to the accepted claims, geography, or website.

## Key findings

1. **Real source-context gains:** Black Focus now has stronger evidence for Quatermass recording in London (artist/engineer testimony plus release credit); Nuova Napoli has a directly credited **cover** of Edition Speciale's Mr.Business and a lyric-text relationship to Eduardo De Filippo; To Pimp A Butterfly has a track-scoped Boris Gardiner source on Wesley's Theory; Moodymann has detailed track- and mix-specific sample evidence. These are candidates for the existing adjudication pipeline, not yet map facts.
2. **Correct refusals:** Infiltrate/Work It remixer, Free Rythms Dance ensemble link, and Elephant session location remain unknown; no geography is imputed from original singers, an association history or suggestive titles.
3. **Granularity matters:** Companionship EP versus original album takes, Sexual Harrassment's later singles versus the Akron original, Universal Beings' four sessions, and Innode's Chicago overdubs versus Vienna recording show why an edition-level pin alone is inadequate.
4. **Source quality gap:** retailers/catalogues can substantiate exact label and track identities, but usually cannot prove where a master was recorded. The method needs explicit grades by claim type and independence of evidence.

## Research-method implications

- Separate **source retrieval** from **source relevance to the exact claim**. An artist bio can prove a scene association but not necessarily a release session city.
- Add a typed `relationship` candidate queue with `sample`, `cover`, `lyric_text`, `contributor`, `influence`, `same_recording`; never collapse these types.
- Track provenance across **recording, overdub, mix, mastering, cutting, manufacture, reissue, and release market**. Retain track/side/version boundaries.
- Re-evaluate success metrics using **claims that would not have been found under the prior method**, with strong and weak source tiers, rather than treating a second corroborating citation as a new discovery.
- Do not score absent evidence as no connection. Keep distinct statuses `verified`, `candidate`, `unresolved`, `contradicted`.
