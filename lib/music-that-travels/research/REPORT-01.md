## Open release questions

- **Beat It / Working Day & Night**: The Beat It guitar contribution is documented; Working Day & Night and other recording contributions need separate research.
- **I Need A Freak**: The original album’s Akron sessions are documented; locate the two later bonus singles separately.
- **Feel My Love**: Locate Room 10 and Eastern Artists studios with independent studio evidence; do not use the label’s Orange County imagery as a recording address.
- **Simurg**: Resolve Esma Ertel’s own musical associations and verify Studio Gam’s location; do not copy Murat Ertel’s history to her.
- **Companionship E.P.**: The white-label EP is not an identified edition of the full Companionship album. Match takes before transferring session credits.
- **Dog**: Locate South Central Studios; only the explicit Berlin re-recording is accepted.
- **The Other Fantasy**: The source names only Europe; city-level sessions remain unresolved.
- **The Aquarius Years**: Current artist location does not establish where early-2000s recordings were made.
- **Your's And Your's Alone / Last Night Changed It All (I Really Had A Ball)**: The owned edition is described as a bootleg. Compare original recordings and sleeve documentation before inheriting detailed production credits.
- **Black Focus**: The Discogs studio credit is present; verify the studio location before adding a recording-place claim.
