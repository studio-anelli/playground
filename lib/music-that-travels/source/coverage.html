Your full collection, several kinds of connection

Loading reviewed musical associations…

Geography loads all 610 collection entries (608 distinct editions). The edition map places entries with known countries or regional markets. The music map gives each entry one overview anchor. Selecting its cover reveals typed, sourced threads: people and scenes, repertoire and traditions, and performances or recordings. Undocumented connections stay unplaced. Both perspectives retain the same collection membership.

Labels distinguish band formation, associated areas, musical scenes, artistic bases and compilation focus. A country association does not establish where a recording was made. Sources include MusicBrainz, artist and label biographies, interviews and compilation documentation. Each placed record links to its sources.

Collection-wide research

Missing connections remain unverified. An unavailable source or an unmatched identity does not mean a record has no cultural connections.

Excluded from the edition map

Excluded records remain in your underlying collection. A record can qualify for one scale and be missing from another.

Colour is a reading, not a fact about the music.

We save average colour, dominant colour and palette shares separately. Their arrangements can reveal different relationships. These measurements use small artwork images, not the physical surface, paper, printing or condition of your copy.

The separate Chromatic Music page retains its 100-record trial while we compare ways to read the full collection’s saved measurements.

Recorded places are a distinct thread

Recording places use documented session sources or explicit “Recorded At” credits matched to a reviewed studio location. Artist birthplaces, genre tags, mastering studios and release countries are not substituted for recording origins. Each recording thread carries source and location evidence in the record’s panel.

Country and city pins are representative positions at that scale. Zooming does not make coarse information more precise.
