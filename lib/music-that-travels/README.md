# Music That Travels · REC-12

Two independent HTML experiences served through a namespaced Next/Vinext route.

- `/experiments/music-that-travels`: entry screen
- `/experiments/music-that-travels/geography`: full saved collection: 610 entries / 608 editions; 600 entries have edition markets, 254 have sourced cultural connections
- `/experiments/music-that-travels/chromatic`: original 100-record artwork colour trial
- `/experiments/music-that-travels/dataset`: coverage and criteria

The saved full collection contains 610 entries / 608 editions. Geography uses every entry; Chromatic keeps its explicitly labelled 100-record study. No collection refresh or colour recalculation runs when opening a study. Copy-specific information remains out of scope.

Sources and provenance are in `source/` and DATASET.md. `source/origin-collection.json` is generated by `python3 lib/music-that-travels/prepare-origins.py` from reviewed sources. `worker.js` is generated by `python3 lib/music-that-travels/build.py`; run it after changing source. The route adapter scopes HTML URLs and APIs to the REC. Image proxy caches are namespaced separately from Playground routes. Discogs artwork is fetched on demand and may be unavailable upstream.

Validation: `node lib/music-that-travels/tests/integration.mjs`, plus dataset/layout tests in `tests/`.

## Loading and rendering

Geography uses one versioned, browser-cacheable display payload (about 517 KB before HTTP compression), excluding the large audit, full credits and unused legacy land data. 604 available overview thumbnails are packed into seven content-addressed WebP sheets (about 806 KB total) in `public/music-that-travels`; four unavailable images retain their frame fallback. Zoomed artwork loads on demand with at most eight preloads at a time. Repack explicitly with `python3 lib/music-that-travels/pack-thumbnails.py` (Pillow required), then rebuild the worker. No artwork download or packing runs during page load or ordinary CI builds.

SVG markers persist during camera changes. Neighbour-cell collision checks and memoized country boundaries replace repeated all-pairs/polygon work. A bounded layout cache reuses zoom bands; pan/wheel updates are batched to animation frames. `tests/performance.mjs` verifies payload size, cache headers, packed assets, full-collection layout timing and national bounds.

## Trackpad camera

Wheel zoom uses normalized pixel magnitude rather than a fixed step per event. Two-finger vertical scrolling gives gentle zoom; browser pinch events (`ctrlKey` wheel) use pinch sensitivity. Safari’s native gesture events are handled separately, without double-counting wheel packets. One animation-frame camera eases toward an accumulated target, keeping the cursor’s map position anchored. Per-event deltas and total scale are bounded. Dragging, perspective changes, reset and journey selection cancel pending motion. Zoom preset buttons use the same interpolation. Cover positions/sizes ease over 240 ms between cached layout bands; closer-level positions remain constrained to national boundaries. `tests/trackpad.mjs` simulates dense tiny events, limits, anchors, settlement and interruption.

## Dense overview rendering

While moving a world-level view with more than 180 eligible entries, reuse the last exact marker/route/traveler paint through a shared affine camera transform. Each motion frame writes the map transform and three overlay transforms instead of walking the cover DOM. At gesture end, drag release, perspective/selection change, or entry into country zoom, clear the temporary transforms and redraw exact positions. Covers and their independent click/keyboard targets remain present. Dense overview artwork disables drop-shadow filters on the large sprite images, avoiding hundreds of filtered surfaces. Persistent marker child references and unchanged attribute checks reduce settled/per-record work. `tests/dense-overview.mjs` checks constant layer writes, mapping and exit conditions.

## Cultural threads

Each collection entry has one overview anchor. Its sourced relationships live in `origin-collection.json.records[id].threads`: stable id, kind (`artist`, `repertoire`, `recording`), relation verb, subject, place/coordinates/precision, source, optional period and note. Legacy artist/compilation associations and reviewed recording evidence are converted by `prepare-origins.py`. `cultural-threads.json` supplies explicitly reviewed artist and release-specific claims. Country repertoire links stay country-level; no nationality or genre inference runs.

The expanded version contains 408 threads across 252 editions / 254 entries. One selected cover reveals typed map endpoints and matching source cards. Selecting a thread highlights and frames it; Show all restores the set. Artist, repertoire, recording and edition-market edges keep different meanings, colours and labels; these are relationships, not a literal travel itinerary. Edition markets retain their separate lens. The map always opens with nothing selected, including links that previously carried a record query. Clicking a cover activates focus and fades the context.

Validation: `tests/cultural-threads.mjs` verifies provenance, kinds, one overview cover per entry, separation of Altın Gün’s Amsterdam base / Turkish repertoire / Swiss edition market, Sun Ra’s ensemble association / Milan performance, and avoidance of title-based artist inheritance. Artist-ID matching keeps Sun Ra and The Sun Ra Arkestra distinct identities with explicit sourced connections.

## Collection-wide research pass

`research-wikidata.py` requests exact P1953 identity cross-links for all 570 named artist IDs (Various and Unknown Artist are excluded from artist-identity inference). `wikidata-collection-evidence.json` retains the returned identity and geographic statements. `musicbrainz-area-evidence.json` records the 221 additional published artist pages checked through those exact P434 cross-links, including unsuccessful and rate-limited outcomes. It contains 129 explicit Area fields, not guessed locations from biography or nationality.

`prepare-collection-research.py` accepts sourced group-formation (P740), work-location (P937), non-human project-origin (P495) and MusicBrainz Artist Area fields, with identity checks and conservative place precision. Birthplaces/citizenship are excluded. `prepare-origins.py` applies these claims across the full collection, alongside the existing reviewed evidence; no remote research happens in the browser.

This pass checks 570 artist IDs, finds 251 identity cross-links, and accepts 244 source-property threads for 160 artists. Combined with the existing evidence, 408 threads place 254 entries / 252 editions. The remaining 356 entries have explicit research reasons in `origin-collection.json.research`: 259 identities need review, 65 linked artists have unverified geography, 30 compilations need curation, and two entries have unidentified artists. Missing evidence does not imply absence of cultural connections. The dataset coverage page displays this audit.

Startup geography includes only display claims, not the research ledger or duplicate legacy metadata (about 623 KB before compression). The full provenance remains in the repository and the audit endpoint.

## Redraw reuse

Overview geographic relationships are resolved once per saved snapshot, then drawn with persistent SVG paths and endpoint circles. Repaints reuse these nodes; layout-only animation frames also skip unchanged route geometry. Selection replaces the overview with interactive thread endpoints and safely restores the persistent overview when cleared. Toolbar descriptions/counts update only when perspective or coverage changes. Cover sizing uses an x-sorted, bounded nearest-neighbour search that produces the same sizes as exhaustive comparisons without scanning every pair. `tests/render-reuse.mjs` checks cache invalidation, selection restoration, constant SVG allocations across 61 redraws, and exact sizes across four zoom scales.

## Redundant place summaries

`group-threads.py` runs during the snapshot build, not during browser interaction. It groups repeat locations for the same subject and thread kind, retaining the most specific display anchor. A country summary can join an existing city/region only when the saved country polygon contains that location. Distinct cities/states/countries remain separate, as do collaborators and the artist/repertoire/recording layers. Unknown containment stays unmerged; country membership is never used to infer state membership. UK constituent countries remain distinct.

The source dataset still contains all 408 original claims. The map presents 303 connections, with the remaining claims available under expandable supporting details that retain their original place, precision, relationship, period, venue and source. A broad US claim is not relabelled as evidence for Chicago. Tests cover Chicago + New York versus a redundant US summary, independent collaborators and cultural layers, and exact preservation of every claim ID.

## Selection placement and collection filters

Selected artwork is a fixed-screen-size overlay (66/84/100 SVG units by zoom level), offset from its geographic anchor with a thin locator guide. Candidate positions avoid the heading, control margins, and nearby thread labels; captions are bounded/truncated with the full record name retained for accessibility. Selection pans only when the anchor is outside the usable area, preserving zoom. Show all includes the edition market in its framing. The dense overview compositor remains active for unselected movement; selected overlays redraw exactly so their size and label bounds remain stable. Native cover tooltips are replaced by the existing custom hover label.

All, Travelling, Same place and Origin unknown are mutually exclusive collection filters, shared across both perspectives. Travelling means at least one documented music relationship differs from the edition market at the available geographic precision. Same place means every known relationship matches; missing origins or missing edition markets remain unknown and never count as local. Origin unknown includes only entries without a documented music connection, independently of whether the edition market is known. Choosing it opens the edition lens so these records remain explorable; the music lens exposes them in the unplaced tray. This is a comparison of cultural relationships and release markets, not a claim about physical shipping. Classifications and subsets are cached per saved snapshot; filter identity is included in layout/route caches. Filtering clears selection; Reset view restores All. Current counts: 128 travelling entries, 124 same-place entries, 356 with no documented origin; two known-origin entries have no placeable edition market.

## Perspective transitions

Switching The music / Your edition snapshots visible cover positions and interpolates to the new layout over 900 ms with cubic easing. Country containment applies to settled layouts, not the intermediate travel path. Newly placeable records fade in; records without a destination fade out rather than being assigned a guessed origin. Selected artwork and its locator guide share the same transition clock. A rapid reversal begins at the current rendered positions. Filters/reset clear transition state, and reduced-motion preferences settle immediately. Dense overview compositing is temporarily paused during the finite transition and resumes for ordinary camera gestures.

## Listening

Sound defaults off. Each selected cover chooses a linked YouTube video automatically. The player sits in an independent dock at the bottom right of the map, below the scrolling details panel. Its visible video viewport is 200 × 200 px, with documented controls=0/fs=0 parameters, an external Play/Pause button and track credit. Branding and overlays are not obscured.

Selection fetches the next track link while the current record keeps playing. After the link is resolved, the existing player fades out over 1.1 seconds, loads the new video in the same iframe, and fades in over 1.4 seconds only after YouTube reports PLAYING. This is a sequential transition, not simultaneous overlapping YouTube playback. A persistent player preserves browser playback permissions and avoids rebuilding an iframe at every cover. Sequence checks cancel superseded requests and fades. Sound off, close/reset and missing tracks stop playback. Unavailable videos try the other links.

`source/listening-catalog.json` stores the collection-wide Discogs link research, including checked records without links and fetch errors. Resume the rate-limited scan with `python3 lib/music-that-travels/collect-listening.py`. `/api/listen/:id` accepts collection IDs only, validates YouTube IDs, ignores explicitly non-embeddable videos, deduplicates and caches results. Edition links take priority; master-release fallback is labelled. No media is downloaded or proxied.

Validation: `tests/listening.mjs`, `tests/listening-transitions.mjs` and `tests/listening-lifecycle.mjs` cover sources, URL validation, cache reuse, fade endpoints/cancellation, persistent player reuse, fetching before fading, state-triggered fade-in, rapid cover replacement and unavailable tracks.

The completed snapshot checks all 608 editions / 610 copies: 587 editions have 2,916 validated linked-video candidates (up to twelve per edition); 21 have no linked video. Four linked editions use a release-family fallback. Every edition lookup is served from the saved catalog without a Discogs request. Run `tests/listening-catalog.mjs` after rebuilding to verify coverage and offline serving. Playback availability remains subject to YouTube.

The selected artwork retains one SVG image node across camera and record changes; cached attributes and image URLs avoid reparsing/loading the same cover on every frame.
